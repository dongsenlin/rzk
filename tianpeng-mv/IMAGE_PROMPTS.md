# 《大天蓬》MV · 男女主角画作提示词

用 Gemini（或任意能生图的工具）生成 8 张图，按下面的顺序来：

1. 先生成 **S2**（男女主角同框），它是之后所有图的「定妆照」；
2. 之后每一张都把 S2 作为参考图一起上传，并在提示词最后加一句：
   `Keep exactly the same two characters (faces, hair, costumes) as in the reference image.`
3. 每张选 **正方形 1:1、能选的最高分辨率（2K 以上最好）**，主体放在画面中央、四周留出雾气或纸面，这样同一张图能同时裁成 16:9 和 9:16。
   如果工具最高只有 1K，就把每张分别按 16:9 和 9:16 各生成一次。
4. 画面里不要有文字、印章、水印；有的话重新生成。

每条提示词 = 共用风格 + 人物设定 + 镜头描述。复制时三段一起粘贴。

## 共用风格（每条都加）
```
Traditional Chinese ink wash painting combined with fine gongbi line work, painted on aged, textured xuan rice paper. Muted grey and charcoal ink washes, dry-brush texture, white ink splatter effects, accents of vermilion red and real gold leaf flakes. Elegant, dramatic, cinematic composition; refined faces and hands. Square 1:1 image, main subject in the central area with generous misty empty margins on all sides. No text, no letters, no seal, no signature, no watermark, no modern elements, not 3D, not anime.
```

## 人物设定（出现谁就加谁）
男主：
```
The man: Marshal Tianpeng in his human form (NOT a pig, no pig features): a handsome celestial general in his late twenties, sharp brows, calm and defiant eyes, long black hair tied in a high topknot with a thin vermilion ribbon, dark charcoal layered robes with subtle gold-leaf armor plates on the shoulders and a gold-trimmed belt. He wields a long nine-toothed iron rake (a rake with nine tines) inlaid with small gold stars.
```
女主：
```
The woman: the spirit born from his rake, an ethereal young woman with long black hair in a half-up bun bound by a vermilion ribbon, gold-leaf hair cuffs, a small vermilion mark on her forehead, flowing white layered robes with a teal inner collar, calm and melancholic eyes, delicate and otherworldly.
```

## 镜头描述

The two stand together on swirling clouds before the colossal Southern Heavenly Gate of the celestial palace under a furious night storm. Jagged lightning splits the dark ink sky. He stands in front gripping the rake, glaring upward in defiance; she stands close behind him, one hand on his shoulder, her white robes and vermilion ribbon whipping in the wind. Low angle, epic scale, grey storm clouds in wet ink washes, gold leaf sparks in the air.
```

Gold leaf flakes drift in the air. Deep charcoal background, mysterious and sacred.
```
