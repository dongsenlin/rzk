# Tesla China careers reel — handoff notes

15 s motion-graphics recruitment spot for https://www.tesla.cn/careers, built as a
showreel piece. Everything is generated by code: picture (Canvas 2D in headless
Chromium), voice (TTS), music and sound design (numpy). If you are a new agent
picking this up, read this file, run `./setup.sh`, and continue from "Remaining work".

## Brief (from the user, in Chinese)
- 15 s, high-energy motion graphics, showreel quality, style and material based on tesla.cn/careers.
- Voice-over: ideally Gemini TTS ("Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS"); a sweet young Mandarin female voice.
  No `GEMINI_API_KEY` exists in the build environment, so the offline voice is used;
  `src/vo_gemini.py` swaps in Gemini once a key is set.
- tesla.cn is blocked by the sandbox egress policy; the look was derived from the
  public Tesla design tokens (Electric Blue `#3E6AE1`, Carbon `#171A20`, white,
  4 px radius buttons, restrained weights) and the 2026 mission
  "建设一个富足非凡的世界" (sustainable transport, renewable energy, AI, robotics).

## Concept: 改变世界，从一条线开始 ("It starts with a line")
One blue line runs through the film and keeps turning into the next thing.
128 BPM, beat = 0.46875 s, 8 bars = exactly 15.0 s. Beat numbers below are `bt(n)`.

## Pipeline
- `src/script.json` — VO lines, per-line speed, and the anchor character + time each line must hit.
- `src/vo_offline.py` → `build/vo/raw/*.wav` (Kokoro v1.1-zh via sherpa-onnx, speaker 50).
  Speaker chosen from all 55 Mandarin female voices by UTMOS naturalness (≈3.8–4.2 on the chosen takes) + SenseVoice ASR check.
- `src/vo_gemini.py` — same output via Gemini TTS (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, auto-picks newest flash TTS model, voice Leda).
- `src/vo_align.py` — SenseVoice per-character timestamps → `build/vo/manifest.json` (absolute char times). The renderer reads it, so text stays in sync whatever voice is used.
- `src/reel.html` + `src/engine.js` + `src/scenes.js` — the film. `engine.js`: easing, text/path helpers, 2 layers (base + half-res emissive), CPU compositor (linear float motion-blur accumulation, bloom, CA, vignette, grain). `scenes.js`: all shots, `drawFrame(t)`, `fxAt(t)`, `samplesAt(t)`.
  Preview in a browser: serve the folder and open `src/reel.html?preview&scale=0.5`.
- `src/render.js` — Playwright renderer (`--stills 1.2,3.4`, `--sheet 0.5`, full render with `--workers N`).
- `src/audio.py` — (todo) music + SFX on the beat grid, VO placement from the manifest, ducking, master → `build/audio.wav`; then mux.
