Which treatment technology should I explore?

Start with the main treatment need: organic load, hydrogen sulfide in biogas, ammonia, suspended solids, or tank mixing. The technology center connects each need with the relevant BIOZOOM equipment family.

What information is useful for an initial discussion?

Share the wastewater or gas source, available analysis, flow rate, treatment targets and any existing equipment. If some information is not yet available, describe what you know so far.

What is the difference between treatment and resource recovery?

Treatment addresses substances in water or gas. Recovery considers useful outputs as well: for example, biological desulfurization removes hydrogen sulfide from biogas and converts it into sulfur. The appropriate route depends on the project.
