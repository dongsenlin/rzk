Process overview

BIOZOOM’s anaerobic reactor portfolio includes IC PLUS, EGSB PLUS and SUPER IC. These families use dual-circulation, external-circulation and super-circulation configurations respectively.

EGSB PLUS builds on the conventional expanded granular sludge bed reactor. Its external circulation provides the upward flow needed to maintain expansion of the granular sludge bed. This circulation promotes mass transfer within the reactor and is designed to improve treatment efficiency.
