Suspended solids and wastewater clarification

Process overview

BIOZOOM’s microsand system is an independently developed flocculation and sedimentation process for wastewater treatment. It is designed to remove suspended solids, chemical oxygen demand, total phosphorus, heavy metals, algae and color.

The system offers an alternative to conventional coagulation, sedimentation and filtration. It complements biological treatment with clarification targeted at these wastewater contaminants.
