Water mixing in tanks and basins

Process overview

MJA jet aerators can be used for water mixing and mounted on the wall of a tank or basin as an alternative to submerged mixers. Their installation can be adapted to the shape of the basin.

Connections can use plastic or steel pipework according to project requirements. This installation arrangement allows the layout to respond to different basin geometries.
