Hydrogen sulfide in biogas

Process overview

BIOZOOM’s biological desulfurization equipment purifies biogas through microbial activity. Microorganisms convert hydrogen sulfide in the gas into sulfur.

The process combines hydrogen-sulfide removal with sulfur recovery. It forms part of BIOZOOM’s focus on anaerobic treatment and related technologies for recovering resources and energy.
