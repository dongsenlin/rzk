Process overview

BIOZOOM develops biological nitrogen-removal equipment for high-ammonia wastewater. The portfolio includes AMX, an anaerobic ammonium-oxidation reactor, and DNR, a biological nitrogen-removal reactor.

AMX and DNR are distinct equipment families within BIOZOOM’s biological treatment offering. They complement the company’s anaerobic wastewater treatment portfolio.
