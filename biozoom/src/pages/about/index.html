Our focus

Anaerobic expertise.
Resource recovery.

BIOZOOM provides integrated treatment solutions for high-strength organic wastewater. Our focus is anaerobic digestion and its related technologies, supporting wastewater treatment alongside resource and energy recovery.

Our reactor portfolio includes dual-circulation IC PLUS, external-circulation EGSB PLUS and super-circulation SUPER IC. Biological desulfurization, nitrogen removal, microsand clarification and jet aeration extend the treatment portfolio.
